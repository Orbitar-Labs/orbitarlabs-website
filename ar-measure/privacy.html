Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 November 2026 · Replaces the version of 24 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Orbitar Labs ("we", "us", "our"), the developer of the AR Measure app for Android on Google Play, package name orbitarlabs.measure.app (the "App"), handles information when you use the App. Orbitar Labs decides how and why the information described here is used, so it is the "data controller" (GDPR), the "business" (California law) and the "data fiduciary" (India's DPDP Act) for it. We built the App so that your measurements and photos stay on your device. The only information that leaves your device automatically is technical crash and diagnostic data, which helps us fix bugs. If you choose to email us, we also receive that email.

In short

  • The App is free. It has no ads, no in-app purchases and no user accounts, and it does not ask for your name, email address, contacts or location.
  • The camera is used only to measure in augmented reality. The live camera image is processed on your device. We never upload or record it.
  • Photos you capture, your saved measurements and your settings are stored only on your device.
  • Crash and diagnostic reports, tied to random installation IDs and not to your name, are sent to Firebase Crashlytics, a Google service that processes them for us.
  • If you use Send feedback or write to us, we receive your email and use it only to help you.
  • We do not sell or share your data or use it for advertising. The App does not use the advertising ID.

1. Information the App uses on your device

Camera

The App asks for camera permission because augmented-reality measuring needs the live camera image. The image and your device's motion-sensor readings are processed on your device, in real time, by Google Play Services for AR (see section 3) to detect surfaces and calculate distances. The App does not record, upload or keep the camera feed, and it does not use ARCore's cloud features (Cloud Anchors or the Geospatial API). You can withdraw camera permission at any time in your device's settings, but the App cannot measure without it.

Photos and saved measurements

When you tap the capture button, the App saves a photo of the screen, including your measurements, together with its name, the time it was taken, a short measurement summary and the tool you used (for example Distance or Height). The photo is stored in the App's private storage and the other details in a small database on your device. We cannot access them. The App does not add location data to photos and does not need access to your device's photo gallery.

Settings

Your chosen language, default unit and whether you have finished onboarding are stored on your device only.

Sharing

A photo or the App's store link is shared only when you choose Share and pick another app (for example, a messaging or email app). What happens next is governed by that app's privacy policy, not ours.

Feedback emails

Settings → Send feedback opens your own email app with a draft addressed to feedback@orbitarlabs.com and the subject "AR Measure feedback" with the App version. The App itself sends nothing: the email leaves your device only if you press Send in your email app, and it travels through your email provider like any other email.

Below the space for your message, the draft contains a "Device info" block to help us reproduce problems: the App version, build type and package name; the Android version and API level; the device manufacturer, brand and model and its processor types (ABIs); screen size, resolution and density; font scale; whether dark mode and animations are on; the App language, system language and layout direction; your chosen unit; whether Google Play Services for AR is available and its version; whether camera permission is granted; the dates the App was installed and last updated; free storage, total memory and whether the device is a low-memory device; how many measurements you have saved with each tool; and your time zone's offset from UTC (for example "UTC+05:30", not your location). It contains no identifiers, account details, IP address, location, photos or measurement values. You can read, change or delete any of it before you send. To fill in the ARCore line, the App asks Google Play Services for AR whether it is available; the first time this runs, Google Play Services for AR may contact Google (see section 3). The App makes no network request of its own for feedback.

When you send it, or write to us at any of our addresses, we receive what you wrote, the device info you left in, anything you attach, and what every email carries: your email address, the name set in your email account and the email's technical headers. We use this only to understand and fix the problem you report and to reply to you; we do not add it to marketing lists or combine it with crash reports. Our email service providers receive and store the messages on our behalf. Retention is described in section 7.

2. Information collected automatically: crash and diagnostic reports

To find and fix problems, the versions of the App we publish on Google Play use Firebase Crashlytics, a service provided by Google LLC. Crashlytics and the Firebase components it depends on (Firebase installations and Firebase sessions) send us:

  • crash details and stack traces (the technical state of the App when it failed), including a short technical note of what the App was doing, for example "saving a photo failed";
  • device information, such as manufacturer and model, Android version, available memory and disk space, screen orientation and the type of network connection;
  • App information, such as the App version, the package name and where the App was installed from;
  • session information: a random session ID and the time the App was opened or brought to the foreground, which are used to work out how many sessions end in a crash;
  • two random identifiers created for your installation of the App: a Crashlytics installation ID and a Firebase installation ID. They are not your advertising ID and are not linked to your name or account, but they do let reports from the same installation be grouped together, so they are personal data under some laws. They are replaced with new ones if you clear the App's data or reinstall the App.

As with any internet connection, Google's servers see your device's IP address when a report is sent. We do not use it to locate or identify you.

Crash reports do not contain your photos, measurements, camera images or feedback emails. We use this data only to diagnose and fix problems and to improve the App's stability. It is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). According to Google, Crashlytics keeps crash stack traces and the identifiers attached to them for 90 days before it starts removing them. The current version of the App does not have a switch to turn crash reporting off; if you do not want this data sent, please do not use the App, and you can ask us to delete reports already sent (section 7). You can read more in Privacy and Security in Firebase and the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Google Play Services for AR (ARCore)

This application runs on Google Play Services for AR (ARCore), which is provided by Google and governed by the Google Privacy Policy. Google Play Services for AR is a separate Google app on your device. Google may collect and process data through it, for example to keep it up to date and working, as described by Google. See how Google Play Services for AR handles your data.

5. How we use and share information, and our legal basis

We use crash and diagnostic data only to keep the App working, to find and fix faults and to improve its stability. We use emails you send us only to handle your request and reply to you. Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we rely on our legitimate interest in providing a stable and secure App and in supporting its users (Article 6(1)(f)); you can object to this at any time (see section 9). You decide whether to email us and what the email contains. Camera processing and the photos you save happen on your device at your request.

We do not sell personal information, we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising, and we do not give it to third parties for their own marketing. The information described above is disclosed only to, and processed by, the service providers that work for us: Google, for crash and diagnostic data, under the Firebase Data Processing and Security Terms, and our email service providers, for emails. We may disclose information if the law requires it, or if the App is transferred to another company, in which case this policy continues to apply to data already collected.

6. Where data is processed

Orbitar Labs is based in India. Google may process crash and diagnostic data in any country where it or its sub-processors have facilities, including the United States. For transfers of personal data from the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, Google relies on the safeguards described in the Firebase terms linked above and in Privacy and Security in Firebase, including the EU-U.S. and Swiss-U.S. Data Privacy Frameworks and the UK Extension, with which Google LLC complies. Emails may be processed in the countries where our email service providers operate.

7. Data retention and deletion

  • Photos and saved measurements: stay on your device until you delete them in the App, clear the App's data, or uninstall the App.
  • Settings: stay on your device until you clear the App's data or uninstall the App.
  • Crash and diagnostic reports: kept by Firebase Crashlytics for 90 days, after which Google starts removing them from its live and backup systems.
  • Emails you send us: kept for up to 24 months after our last exchange with you, then deleted, or sooner if you ask.

The App does not back up your data to the cloud. Because we do not hold your photos or settings, uninstalling the App removes them permanently, and nobody can restore them.

How to ask us to delete data. Email support@orbitarlabs.com with the subject "Delete my data". Because crash data is not linked to your name or email, please tell us your device model, the App version and the approximate date and time of the crash or of your last use, so that we can look for the matching reports. To delete emails you sent us, write from the same email address; we will delete them and our replies. We will delete what we can identify and reply within 30 days. If we cannot identify your crash reports, they are deleted automatically at the end of the 90-day period above, and clearing the App's data or uninstalling the App stops any link to future reports. You do not need an account to make a request.

8. Security

Your photos, saved measurements and settings are kept in the App's private storage, which other apps cannot read, and are excluded from device backups. Crash and diagnostic data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and the App does not allow unencrypted network traffic. Access to crash reports and to our mailboxes is limited to the people at Orbitar Labs who need it to fix problems and answer you. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect information.

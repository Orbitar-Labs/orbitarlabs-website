Our vision

Useful apps and fun games that people can trust, every day.

Our mission

We design and build focused mobile apps that solve everyday problems well, and games that are a pleasure to play. Everything we make should be easy to pick up, work reliably on the phones people actually own, and respect their privacy.

Our goals

  • Quality first. Fast, stable apps and games that run smoothly on budget and flagship phones alike.
  • Privacy by default. Collect only what an app needs, and explain clearly what and why.
  • Built with our users. Regular updates shaped by the feedback people send us.

What we offer

We make mobile apps and games for everyday people. We take each one from the first sketch to the app store, and keep looking after it once people are using it.

  • 02

  • 03

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 20 November 2026

In short:

  • This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no advertising trackers.
  • If you email us, we use your message and email address only to reply and to keep a record of the conversation.
  • We do not sell personal information.
  • Each of our apps and games has its own privacy policy for what it does on your phone, linked from its Google Play listing.

1. Who we are

Orbitar Labs ("Orbitar Labs", "we", "us") designs, builds and supports mobile apps and games.

Orbitar Labs is responsible for the personal information described in this policy. You can reach us about privacy at info@orbitarlabs.com.

2. What this policy covers

This policy applies to:

  • the website at orbitarlabs.com;
  • email you exchange with us at an @orbitarlabs.com address; and
  • mobile apps and games published by Orbitar Labs, for example on Google Play, where that app or game does not have its own privacy policy.

3. Information we collect

On this website

We do not ask you to create an account or fill in a form, and we do not set cookies or run analytics or advertising scripts on this website.

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub records technical information when a page is requested, such as the visitor's IP address, the date and time, and the browser type, in order to deliver the site and keep it secure. We do not receive these logs. GitHub's handling of this information is described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

When you email us

If you write to us, we receive your email address, your name if you provide it, and whatever you choose to include in your message, such as a description of a problem or a screenshot. Please do not send sensitive information such as passwords, payment card details or government ID numbers.

In our apps and games

Each app or game we publish has its own privacy policy, linked from its Google Play listing and from inside the app. It explains what information that app accesses, collects or shares, why, and how you can ask for it to be deleted, and the same information appears in the app's Data safety section on Google Play. Where an app's own policy and this page differ, the app's own policy applies to that app.

For every app we publish, we follow these principles:

  • We collect only what an app needs in order to work.
  • Access to sensitive device features, such as the camera, location or files, is requested through your device's permission prompts, and you can withdraw a permission at any time in your device settings.
  • We do not sell personal information.

When you install an app from Google Play, Google also processes information under its own privacy policy.

4. How we use information

We use the information described above to:

  • reply to your questions and requests;
  • provide support for our apps and games and fix problems you report;
  • keep a record of our correspondence; and
  • meet legal obligations and protect our rights and the rights of our users.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not send marketing email.

5. Who we share information with

We do not sell or rent personal information. We rely on a small number of service providers that process information on our behalf:

  • GitHub, which hosts this website;
  • ImprovMX, which forwards email sent to our @orbitarlabs.com addresses; and
  • Google, which provides our mailbox (Gmail) and distributes our apps and games (Google Play).

These providers may process information on servers outside India. We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or if it is necessary to protect the safety, rights or property of our users, the public or Orbitar Labs.

6. How long we keep information

We keep email correspondence for as long as it is needed to deal with your request and for a reasonable period afterwards for our records, unless you ask us to delete it sooner or the law requires us to keep it longer.

7. Security

This website is served over HTTPS. We limit access to our mailbox and accounts and protect them with strong passwords and two-step verification. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your choices and rights

You can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal information we hold about you;
  • correct information that is inaccurate or out of date;
  • delete your information; or
  • stop using your information, where our use is based on your consent.

To make a request, or to raise a concern or complaint about how we handle your information, email info@orbitarlabs.com. We will reply within 30 days. Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

9. Children

This website is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children through it. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We will update this policy when our practices change, including before we release a new app. The effective date at the top of the page shows when it was last revised. Significant changes will be noted on this page.

11. Contact us

If you have any questions about this policy or how we handle your information, email us at info@orbitarlabs.com.
